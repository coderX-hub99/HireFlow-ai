# 🚀 Overview

**HireFlow AI** is an agentic AI recruitment intelligence system designed to assist recruiters in screening candidates and conducting evidence-driven interviews.

Instead of simply assigning a candidate a numerical score, HireFlow analyzes the relationship between:

```text
Job Requirements
        ↓
Candidate Evidence
        ↓
Requirement Validation
        ↓
Missing / Unclear Information
        ↓
Targeted Interview Questions
        ↓
Candidate Responses
        ↓
Evidence Evaluation
        ↓
Follow-up Questions
        ↓
Final Evidence-Based Report
